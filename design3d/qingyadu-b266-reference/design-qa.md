## Iteration notes

1. Initial build: all 3D component identity/bounds, day/night, animation, movement, orbit, reset, mobile overflow, and network-byte checks passed. Visual comparison found the inn too low/flat, tea stall too far left, trees and dock misaligned, and water too etched.
2. Revision build: raised/scaled the inn, moved gate/tea/trees/dock/boats, added `客栈`, canopy `茶`, and vertical `清茶一盏`, switched to detail-2 recipes, and softened the water treatment. Visual comparison found the water slab too wide.
3. Final build: reduced water slab to restore the source's native margin while keeping the improved architecture and sign hierarchy. All managed Chrome checks passed again.

## Remaining differences

The final 3D audit retains major findings for painterly-vs-low-poly shading and simplified inn/awning/interior detail. Minor findings remain for regular masonry, foliage clusters, wicker baskets, and water reflections. The audit reports `requires_revision=true`, `workflow_complete=false`, and no certificate because the development service deliberately gates silhouette/occlusion acceptance, production worker attestation, and calibrated visual thresholds.
